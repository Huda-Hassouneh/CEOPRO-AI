# KEEL — Refund and Billing Dispute Policy (Draft)

> **DRAFT 0.1 — NOT APPROVED.** Do not publish a universal “no refunds” claim or an unconditional refund promise. The payment provider and applicable law matter.

## 1. Scope and seller identification
This notice explains where customers can request a refund, report an incorrect charge or ask for billing assistance. The actual seller/Merchant of Record depends on the payment provider shown in the specific order confirmation. Operator contact: **keel.business.ai@gmail.com**.

## 2. Paddle transactions
For purchases for which Paddle is Merchant of Record, Paddle's current Buyer Terms and Refund Policy govern the transaction, subject to non-waivable rights. Customers may request help or a refund through **https://paddle.net** and any link in the Paddle receipt or customer portal. KEEL can help investigate software problems, but must not promise to issue a Paddle-transaction refund directly or override Paddle's governing requirements.

## 3. Non-Paddle transactions
For orders sold directly by the correctly identified KEEL operator through another gateway, refunds are evaluated under **the approved intention to review refund requests within 14 days after the first paid charge, without a guaranteed refund and subject to applicable law and seller/provider obligations**, the transaction's accepted terms, and mandatory applicable law. These rules require separate owner/legal approval before live sales.

Submit a review request to **keel.business.ai@gmail.com** within 14 days after the first paid charge, including the order reference, first-charge date and reason. Do not email card numbers, passwords or other payment secrets. For a Paddle order, use the original receipt or Paddle buyer support as well. The request window is not a guarantee of eligibility, approval or processing time and does not limit mandatory rights or applicable Paddle obligations.

## 4. Problems with service
Customers should report persistent failures or materially inaccessible purchased functionality to **keel.business.ai@gmail.com** with the relevant invoice/transaction reference and a description of the problem. We will review the issue and coordinate with the appropriate Merchant of Record as required. Mandatory consumer remedies remain available.

## 5. Cancellation versus refund
Cancelling a subscription generally prevents future renewals according to the accepted order and provider terms; it does not automatically reverse prior completed payments. Free trial and cancellation deadlines must be presented before acceptance and are **[[APPROVED_TRIAL_AND_CANCELLATION_POLICY]]**.

## 6. Processing and disputes
Refund approval, method and timing depend on the Merchant of Record and law; do not promise a fixed number of days without verification. Customers should contact support or the provider's dispute channel before submitting repeated requests. Nothing here removes a lawful right to dispute an unauthorized charge.

## 7. Updates
Policy version, effective date and dispute contact: **[[REFUND_POLICY_APPROVAL_METADATA]]**. If required, notify the payment provider of changes.
